## Top priorities

Scope: OpenStack 2017-05-14 to 05-17, levels WARNING, ERROR and CRITICAL (3,314 lines). BGL 2005-06-03 to 2006-01-04, levels WARNING, SEVERE, ERROR, FAILURE and FATAL (1,011,834 lines). All counts are log lines, not incidents.

**1. BGL node cards not fully working (HPC Hardware Ops)**
- OBSERVED: "Node card is not fully functional" appears 17,364 times across 2,002 nodes, and "Can not get assembly information" 17,305 times across 2,000 nodes. Both run from Jun 8 to Dec 29, so it persists to the end of the data.
- Action: audit the node-card inventory and the failing cards.
- Confidence: high that it is widespread; low on what causes it.

**2. Two single-source floods in BGL**
- OBSERVED: "data TLB error interrupt" is the largest pattern at 152,734 lines (15.09%). Of those, 152,182 came from one node in the week of 2005-06-06. A control-system ASSERT accounts for 84,168 lines (8.32%), all from one node, in bursts in August and September.
- HYPOTHESIS: fixing two sources would remove about 23% of the error noise. Owners are HPC Node Software (TLB errors) and HPC Control System (ASSERT).
- Action: find and fix those two nodes or processes.
- Confidence: high.

**3. OpenStack image cache warnings and failures (Storage & Images, Platform Runtime)**
- OBSERVED: "Unknown base file" makes up 92.31% of OpenStack warnings and errors (3,059 lines), on every day that has data. The image-cache cleanup job failed 8 times.
- OBSERVED: the 4 labelled problem VMs took 38.3 s on average to start, against 19.8 s for the other 2,062.
- Action: check that the image cache's base files are consistent.
- Confidence: medium. Low on the slow starts, since only 4 VMs are involved.

## What we don't know yet
- Whether any of these affected jobs or users. The logs have no impact data. NEXT CHECK: match the times against job and ticket records.
- Which node produced each flood. NEXT CHECK: group those two patterns by node.
- Whether "Unknown base file" is harmless. NEXT CHECK: compare it with VM failures.
- What causes the slow VM starts. NEXT CHECK: look at the event timeline of those 4 VMs.
