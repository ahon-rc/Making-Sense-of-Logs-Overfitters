## Top priorities

**1. VM image cache errors on OpenStack (Storage & Images)**
- OBSERVED: "Unknown base file" makes up 3,059 of 3,314 non-INFO lines (92.31%). It appears in every hour of data, with a median of 128 lines per hour, including the "normal" periods. Separately, the image-cache cleanup job failed 8 times (Platform Runtime). The logs tie these lines to no individual VM.
- HYPOTHESIS: this is a steady, ongoing problem, not a one-off. It may be leftover image files or just noise.
- Action: Storage & Images should find out whether these files are orphaned and why the cleanup job fails. Confidence: **high** on frequency, **low** on impact.

**2. Memory-access (TLB) error flood from one BGL node (HPC Node Software)**
- OBSERVED: 152,734 lines across 541 nodes. Of these, 152,182 came from 1 node in the week of 2005-06-06. Every other week had 477 lines or fewer.
- HYPOTHESIS: one faulty node, not a fleet-wide problem.
- Action: NEXT CHECK: identify that node and review its hardware history. Confidence: **medium**.

**3. Repeated control-software crash check (ASSERT) on BGL (HPC Control System)**
- OBSERVED: 84,168 lines (8.32% of non-INFO) from 1 node, 2005-08-03 to 2005-09-20.
- Action: NEXT CHECK: Control System should look for a matching software defect or fix. Confidence: **medium**.

## What we don't know yet
- Impact: there are no outage, user or SLA signals in the logs. Linking these errors to failed jobs or VMs would need ticket or monitoring data.
- Slow VM starts: OBSERVED: the 4 labelled problem VMs took 38.3s on average to start, against 19.8s for the other 2,062. With only 4 cases, the cause is unclear (HYPOTHESIS: possibly linked to priority 1).
- BGL Lustre mount failures (26,426 lines on 1,024 nodes) were not investigated further.

Scope: OpenStack 2017-05-14 to 05-17 (WARNING, ERROR, CRITICAL). BGL 2005-06-03 to 2006-01-04 (WARNING through FATAL). Counts are log lines, not incidents. The message-pattern lists show only the top 8 groups.
